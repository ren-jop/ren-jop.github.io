Engineering problem

Procedural survival systems only work if generated spaces remain traversable and the player can understand why their state is changing. The project became an exercise in connecting systemic depth to clear feedback.

Movement and generation

Movement includes gravity, collision handling, coyote time and jump buffering. The cave generator was redesigned after testing exposed impossible jumps, so variation is built around a guaranteed reachable path.

Survival loop

Injuries, bleeding, fractures, hunger and thirst interact with inventory, crafting, enemies and hazards. Systems that added complexity without useful decisions—such as the original weight model—were simplified after testing.

Current direction

The newer gameplay pass adds a clearer tutorial, compact HUD, hotbar/crafting flow, hazards and extraction objective, with regression review focused on player-facing clarity rather than adding more isolated features.
