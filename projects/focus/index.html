Focus

Focus is a native macOS menu-bar timer by Ren Jopson with configurable Pomodoro timing, count-up sessions and work history.

What it does

• Configurable focus, short-break and long-break durations
• Countdown and open-ended count-up timer modes
• Low-overhead menu-bar status with local JSONL history
• Planner and Deadlock integration for planned-vs-actual sessions and distraction protection
